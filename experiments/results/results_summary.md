# Experiment results

Generated by `python/analyse_results.py` from the native simulation. Traces are synthetic, generated with one fixed seed (42); see `seed_study.md` for a multi-seed check of the water profile. Energy is an estimate from the model in `firmware/src/energy.c`, not a measurement.

Totals and means are aggregated over all 8 scenarios of each profile. A true *event* merges critical-limit crossings closer than 1800 s; *crossings* counts every excursion separately. Detection tolerance: 600 s.

`recon_err` is the RMS difference between what the gateway could display at each moment (packets already received) and the noise-free truth, in units of the channel's `delta_report`; `recon_err_offline` uses every packet at its sample time, i.e. after queued data has arrived.
